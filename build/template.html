Relation satisfaction in this scene

RSR is the fraction of in-scope frames whose signed geometric margin is satisfied. Ground truth sits near 1.0 rather than at it: the dataset quality gate accepts a relation at 0.95, so speed transitions and boundary frames are legitimately shaved.

What the relation score cannot see

A planner that optimises the declared relations can satisfy them and still produce geometry no listener would accept. Head clearance is the share of frames with a source closer than 0.5 m to the listener, and the last column is the closest any source ever gets. Our own weak column is smoothness: the diffusion model is jerkier than the ground truth, which we report rather than hide.

What the system can be told

Relations and motions

Descriptions are written in a controlled relational language. Relations either place a source with respect to the listener or tie two sources to each other; the inter-source family is the one this work is about. Counts are relation instances across the whole dataset.

Held-out motion–relation combinations

Five combinations were frozen out of training before any model was trained. Each keeps a sibling in training, so what is tested is composition, not an unseen relation type.

Text to conditioning

The language model in the pipeline

End-to-end RSR is the trajectory score when the model is conditioned on the parse instead of the ground-truth graph, on the same held-out scenes. Note that the LLM-direct baseline in the viewer above is a different thing: there, a general model is asked for coordinates directly, with no graph, no anchors and no trajectory model.

Reproducibility

How these clips were made

Nothing on this page is a hand-picked sample. The twelve scenes were chosen by a rule fixed before any system was run: every scene must have several sources, at least one inter-source relation, two distinguishable timbres for the related pair and clean ground truth; among those, scenes were taken greedily so that all seven inter-source relations and as many motion primitives as possible appear. The clip you hear is always the first draw under the pre-registered per-scene seed.

On the baselines. No previously published system was run for this page. The independent per-source column is our own model with the cross-source information flow structurally removed and the anchor tokens withheld — a single-variable control that isolates what joint conditioning contributes, and the closest learned stand-in for generating each source on its own. The rule solver optimises the declared relations directly with a constraint solver, and LLM-direct asks a general language model for coordinates. Each is a construction of ours; none carries anyone else's name, and none should be read as a measurement of another group's method.
